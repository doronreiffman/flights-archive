# data.gov.il terms, as read on 2026-10-29 (q12)

My call, 2026-10-29, on my reading of the terms (not legal advice): they allow publishing the history timeline (derived per-flight pages). My call, 2026-11-17, on the same reading, with the terms page's update date still 30/08/2025: they also allow publishing rates over past flights (how often flights with a given time to go were later listed as cancelled, `docs/flight-lookup.md`). My call, also 2026-11-17, on the same reading: they also allow what the "Will It Fly?" pages show: a chance for one flight (worked out from its own record, its airline's and all flights at the airport, and how soon it leaves), one airline's record, and delays of an hour or more by the airport's own expected time, presented as the clause-4 reading below sets out. Raw replies are not published; on the same reading the general license would allow that too, but nothing needs it.

## Clauses that bear on this project

Hebrew as on the page; the English is ours.

1. Precedence (intro): "ככל שלמאגר מידע מוצמד רישיון שימוש השונה מהרישיון המובא להלן, יגבר הרישיון המוצמד למאגר המידע." Where a dataset carries its own license that differs from this one, the dataset's license wins. flydata's "Other (Open)" label has no text, so nothing on record differs; we read the general license as governing (our inference).
2. Changes ("רישיון לשימוש במאגרי המידע באתר Data.Gov.il"): "המשרד רשאי לשנות את תנאי רישיון זה בכל עת, כאשר כל שינוי כזה ייכנס לתוקף מידית עם הצגתו באתר. השימוש במידע יהיה בכפוף לתנאי הרישיון שהיו בתוקף בתאריך ובשעה בהם נלקח המידע מהאתר." The Office may change the license at any time, effective as soon as it is posted; use of the information is subject to the terms in force on the date and at the time it was taken.

You may copy, distribute, make public, broadcast, change technically and make derivative works, in any medium or format, commercially or not.
4. Prohibited use 1 ("שימושים אסורים"): "להציג את המידע באופן מטעה ו/או באופן הגורם למצג שווא ו/או לשנות את המידע בדרך שתגרום לסילופו ו/או באופן הפוגע או העלול לפגוע בכבודו או בשמו של בעל זכויות היוצרים בו." Presenting the information misleadingly or so as to create a false impression, changing it so as to distort it, or in a way that harms or may harm the honour or reputation of its copyright holder.

6. Cite the source ("ציון מקור המידע"): "בשימוש במידע, עליך לציין את מקור המידע." When using the information, you must cite its source. No wording is prescribed.

Information whose publication or access is forbidden by law, or that was obtained unlawfully or without permission, including by circumventing technological measures meant to protect it.

No official status or endorsement ("העדר גושפנקא או חסות"): "רישיון זה אינו מקנה לך זכות להשתמש במידע באופן היוצר מצג או מרמז על כל מעמד רשמי בשימוש זה או על כך שהמדינה מעניקה חסות או נותנת גושפנקא לך, לצד שלישי, או לשימוש שאתה או צד שלישי עושים במידע או בכל מוצר ערך מוסף." ## What it asks of us (our reading)

- Cite the source (clause 6): every page carries `SOURCE_SHORT` ("Flight data: Israel Airports Authority, published on data.gov.il.", `src/timeline/words.py`), and `how-it-works.html` also the full `ATTRIBUTION` (`src/model/report.py`, with the dataset and resource ID); all 959 pages of the 2026-10-30 build carry theirs.
- No implied official status or endorsement (clause 8): every page says "This site is unofficial: it isn't run by, connected to or approved by the Israel Airports Authority, data.gov.il or the State of Israel." (`UNOFFICIAL`, `src/timeline/words.py`). `scripts/publish.sh` runs `src/timeline/check.py`, which refuses a site where any page lacks it or `SOURCE_SHORT`, or where `how-it-works.html` lacks `ATTRIBUTION`, so a build made before the current wording cannot go out.
- No misleading presentation (clause 4): my reading, 2026-11-17, for the "Will It Fly?" pages. The site meets it when every figure says what it is and what it rests on.
  - A chance is an estimate from past flights, not a promise. Every page says so in its footer and links to "What the numbers mean". That section defines "cancelled" (the airport's list marked the flight cancelled) and "delayed" (the airport's own expected time was an hour or more after the scheduled time).
  - A flight page that gives a chance says what it was worked out from: the flight's own history with its counts, its airline's history, and how soon it leaves. History lines give counts, not percentages.
  - A chance is never "0%". Below 1%, it reads "under 1%".
  - When one bad week is behind a flight's or an airline's cancellations, the page gives its dates.
  - Delays under an hour are never counted, because the airport doesn't say whether its times mean leaving the gate or taking off.
  - Every page says when it was last updated, and a flight page tells the reader to check with their airline.
  - A cancellation never gets a clock time. How much notice it gave is a range or a bound, because only the time the list first showed it cancelled is known. "Cancelled at" would be misleading.
  - The ranges behind the figures are on "How this works", not on the traveller's pages.

  Before every upload, `scripts/publish.sh` runs `src/timeline/check.py` on the real site. In every page, and in the style sheet and the scripts, it refuses the banned phrases (`BANNED`: "cancelled at", "delayed by", "took off", "minutes late" and others), the built-by placeholder, and "we", "our" and "us". Outside `how-it-works.html` it refuses "I" (except in the built-by line), data-collection words such as "board", "snapshot" and "observed", and every odds word but "chance" and "chances" (odds, probability, likely, risk, forecast, predict and their forms). It requires the source line, the unofficial line and the estimates line ("The chances on this site are estimates from past flights, not promises.") on every page, the full attribution on `how-it-works.html`, and a "Worked out from" line on every flight page that gives a chance. The word rules and the odds words skip the airport's own strings, so an airline named "US-BANGLA AIRLINES" cannot stop an upload; the banned-phrase rule still reads them. `tests/test_timeline_check.py` tests the check; `tests/test_timeline_build.py` also runs `BANNED` over a synthetic build.
- Privacy (clause 5): the board holds no personal data (the fields under "Columns" in `docs/data-model.md`, mapped to the board's own names in `COLUMNS`, `src/model/rules.py`), and we cross-reference it with nothing.
- No emblems or logos (clause 9): the timeline's anti-goals already forbid logos.
- Blocks (clause 7): the collector stops at the first block (401, 403, 429, or an HTML page where JSON belongs) and, once its latch is saved, waits for a person, so it never works around one.
- Changes (clause 2): before each upload, I check the terms page's update date in my browser; if it has moved past 30/08/2025, the terms are read again before uploading.
