## Clauses that bear on this project

Hebrew as on the page; the English is ours.

1. Precedence (intro): "ככל שלמאגר מידע מוצמד רישיון שימוש השונה מהרישיון המובא להלן, יגבר הרישיון המוצמד למאגר המידע." Where a dataset carries its own license that differs from this one, the dataset's license wins. flydata's "Other (Open)" label has no text, so nothing on record differs; we read the general license as governing (our inference).

## What it asks of us (our reading)

- Cite the source (clause 6): every page carries `SOURCE_SHORT` ("Flight data: Israel Airports Authority, published on data.gov.il.", `src/timeline/words.py`), and `how-it-works.html` also the full `ATTRIBUTION` (`src/model/report.py`, with the dataset and resource ID); all 959 pages of the 2026-10-30 build carry theirs.
- No implied official status or endorsement (clause 8): every page says "Unofficial; not affiliated with or endorsed by the Israel Airports Authority, data.gov.il or the State of Israel." (`UNOFFICIAL`, `src/timeline/words.py`). `scripts/publish.sh` runs `src/timeline/check.py`, which refuses a site where any page lacks it or `SOURCE_SHORT`, or where `how-it-works.html` lacks `ATTRIBUTION`, so a build made before the current wording cannot go out.
- No misleading presentation (clause 4): we read the "Words" rules of `docs/site-redesign.md` as how we meet it: a warning is a range or a lower bound, a cancellation's time only ever a range, the headline says "listed as cancelled", and expected times are never turned into delay figures. A page saying "cancelled at" where we only know when the list first showed the flight cancelled would be misleading. Before every upload, `scripts/publish.sh` runs `src/timeline/check.py` on the real site: it refuses the banned phrases (`BANNED`: "cancelled at", "delayed by", "took off", "minutes late" and others), "we", "our" and "us", and, outside `how-it-works.html`, "I" (except in the built-by line) and data-collection words such as "board", "snapshot" and "observed". The airport's own strings are skipped, so an airline named "US-BANGLA AIRLINES" cannot stop an upload. `tests/test_timeline_check.py` tests the check; `tests/test_timeline_build.py` also runs `BANNED` over a synthetic build.
- Privacy (clause 5): the board holds no personal data (the fields in CLAUDE.md, "The data"), and we cross-reference it with nothing.
- No emblems or logos (clause 9): the timeline's anti-goals already forbid logos.
- Blocks (clause 7): the collector stops at the first block (401, 403, 429, or an HTML page where JSON belongs) and, once its latch is saved, waits for a person, so it never works around one.
- Changes (clause 2): before each upload, the owner checks the terms page's update date in their browser; if it has moved past 30/08/2025, the terms are read again before uploading.
